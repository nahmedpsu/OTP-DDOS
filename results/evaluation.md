# Evaluation

Generated by `scripts/run_evaluation.py`: 2640 simulation runs, 30 seeds for the main study and 10 for sweeps, 235 s wall time. Values are means with 95 % t-confidence intervals in brackets. Metric definitions and limitations: `docs/evaluation.md`.

Each run: 10 minutes of legitimate-only warm-up, then 20 minutes of attack with legitimate traffic (20 requests/min, 80 % conversion, verification delay lognormal median 25 s) in the background. Per seed the attacker's pool size (500 to 50 000 addresses), rate (10 to 60 requests/min) and CAPTCHA score class are randomised. Modes: **behavioural only** lifts the source caps so the other layers are visible; **with adaptive caps** runs the source cap at 3x the legitimate rate with the adaptive baseline job.
