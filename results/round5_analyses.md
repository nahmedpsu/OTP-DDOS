## D. Operating condition: a heuristic comparison of two simplified estimates (M5; sixth round M2, M3)

Security map (E4, leaked SMS, means over 5 seeds). Two estimates with different horizons are set beside the measured leakage. The counter's allowance is q x B x w over the whole attack (q = 4 sends per block per 10-minute window, B blocks, w windows: 2 in 20 minutes, 6 in 60), capped at N, the volume the pumper offered in the counter arm (a quota-aware pumper offers about the allowance, so its cap binds); the envelope is an upper bound on what the counter can let through, not a prediction, and it assumes the pumper stays subject to the quota (no verified-history exemption). The sequential tests' figure is Equation 2's leakage before the first verdict, k x B + lambda x tau with k = 5, capped at N in the sequential arm; it does not describe containment, what happens after a verdict expires, or a campaign that continues after it. Its intercept lambda x tau = 95 is FITTED on one cell (3 blocks, spreading, never verifies, 60 minutes: 110 leaked minus k x 3); agreement on that row is imposed, every other row is a check. The five sampled spreads bracket the crossing between 3 and 10 blocks for a 60-minute campaign and do not locate it more precisely.

Service model: share of a block's sends beyond the quota, the requests a graded counter challenges (beyond q) or moves off SMS (beyond 2q), under two window models. *Exogenous*: fixed 10-minute windows open whether or not anything arrives, n ~ Poisson(m) sends per window, share E[(n - q)+] / m. *Arrival-anchored* (the implementation: `try_reserve_tiered` sets the window's TTL on the first accepted reservation and later reservations do not restart it): a reset cycle holds the send that opened it plus X ~ Poisson(m) sends in its duration, share E[(1 + X - q)+] / (1 + m). Both are idealised diagnostics (homogeneous arrivals, every request reaching the counter, no exemptions, no earlier gates or challenge failures), not measurements of the simulator; the arrival-anchored column is the one the deployed counter corresponds to.
